Original

The kettle was arguing with the rain. Before the bus came, when my aunt had already put the spoons in the drawer, I arrived at the house, soaked and late. She laughed. That was how our Thursdays began.

The agent’s revision

The kettle was arguing with the rain. I arrived at the house, soaked and late, before the bus came and after my aunt had put the spoons in the drawer. She laughed. That was how our Thursdays began.

What changed: the arrival comes first in the second sentence. The unusual image survives. The edit was generated; whether readers find it clearer remains untested.

What this supports.

On this suite, the skill usually preserved intent, kept taste and elegance separate, checked critical requirements, and bounded improvement. A local rollback exercise restored intentionally repeated names after a cleanup removed them; independent replay confirmed the failure and the restoration.

What remains unestablished: objective aesthetic quality, audience benefit, automatic memory, or reliability across models and real-world tasks. These are curated cases, with one run per condition in one model environment. Some initial cases resemble the calibration examples. Controls often gave useful answers too; the rubric includes the skill’s own preferred process, so these small comparisons do not establish general superiority.

The task agents received bounded read/write instructions, not separate OS sandboxes. Code behavior was replayed locally where applicable; execution logs elsewhere are agent-reported. Reviewer agreement describes consistency, not independent human validation.
