# Methodology review

Scope: read only PLAN.md, manifest.json, continuations.json, snapshot/SKILL.md, and snapshot/references/calibration.md. No task responses, evaluator judgments, execution logs, other repository files, or memory were inspected. These are design findings, not response grades. No design changes are proposed for this completed/frozen run.

## What the design supports

- A traceable, curated assessment of observable compliance with this exact skill on 12 distinct initial cases, plus two explicit handoff exercises. Frozen prompts/checks, independent grading, evidence quotations, execution-log review, and reporting failed runs are useful safeguards if implemented as specified.
- Four matched prompts support descriptive within-prompt comparisons on C01, C02, C03, and C09. They do not identify a reliable average treatment effect, general superiority, or reliability rate. Platform instructions and base-model competence can explain success in either condition.
- Correct code checks can establish behavior only on executed inputs. They cannot establish audience comprehension, maintainability, aesthetic improvement, or the validity of numerical coordinates. The plan correctly distinguishes these.
- Continuations test attending to a supplied note and explicit correction. They do not test automatic memory, locating a saved artifact, durable persistence, or a longitudinal user collaboration.

## Concrete coverage gaps and confounds

1. **Strong prompt cueing and example proximity.** Many prompts state the desired boundary directly: keep an odd phrase, retain density, do not extend budget, no study, no publication. C02 closely resembles the calibration's family-story example; C03 resembles its neighborhood-workshop reframe; C01 resembles its branch-removal formatter example. Results primarily demonstrate near-transfer and compliance under helpful cues, not spontaneous diagnosis or generalization to unfamiliar situations.
2. **Limited condition coverage.** The eight remaining initial cases and both continuations lack matched controls. The design cannot attribute their behavior specifically to the skill. The four pairs also combine many skill instructions, so they cannot isolate which component causes any difference.
3. **Low operational exposure.** Most tasks request advice or explicitly forbid action; there is no authorized artifact-editing workflow with baseline preservation, actual rollback, failed recheck, or a consequential scope transition. Abstaining from prohibited deployment is useful evidence, but weak evidence of managing real deployment authorization or recovery.
4. **Breadth within the skill remains thin.** There is only one requested matrix case; no direct test requires accepting a decrease on one axis, maintaining axis disagreement across alternatives, defending purposeful friction, or choosing between two genuinely plausible routes under ambiguous evidence. Sparse-intent elicitation is a single reply, with no user answer. There is no audience-feedback loop or tension between user resonance and conflicting audience evidence.
5. **Continuation dependence and provenance.** H01 and H02 reuse the same C01-derived note and current code. They are two variations of one handoff, not independent replications. The plan announces continuations before generation, but these exact prompts contain response-derived note text; their exact bytes cannot have been frozen before that source response existed. Report their freeze timing and derivation separately from the initial manifest without treating them as preregistered independent observations. The provided files alone do not establish whether extraction followed a predeclared rule.
6. **Sampling and implementation uncertainty.** One run per case, unavailable seed/temperature control, one model family, and imperfect masking preclude stability estimates. The plan does not specify run order/randomization, evaluator identities/settings or independence mechanism, or exact treatment/control wrapper text. These omissions constrain interpretation; the artifacts inspected do not prove a procedural violation.

## Rubric issues to disclose when interpreting scores

- Several checks bundle multiple obligations (for example C01's two steps, checks, rollback, rationale; C03's experiment, safety, feasibility, stop). Pass/partial/fail thresholds are not operationally defined. Two graders can disagree about whether one missing subpart warrants partial or fail. Report reasons and adjudication rather than reading labels as precise measurements.
- Case pass is conjunctive across all applicable checks; case results therefore depend on how requirements were grouped. This is an exacting compliance outcome, not a calibrated quality scale. Not-applicable discretion also needs visibility in the report.
- Some acceptance checks demand skill-specific process beyond the user's explicit brief, including rollback/revert, stopping language, and decision rationale. Applying them to controls tests whether the skill induces its own preferred process as well as whether answers are useful. It is not a neutral comparison of user-perceived response quality; excess ritual can also conflict with the shared proportionality check.
- “Plausible worse,” “actual better,” “warm voice,” “friendly,” “proportionate,” and “useful” require judgment. Opaque IDs do not fully blind graders because skill vocabulary and structure can disclose condition. Agreement measures evaluator consistency, not external validity or human aesthetic consensus.
- C02 asks for an exact lowercase quoted phrase while its supplied draft starts the same phrase with an uppercase letter. Literal matching can penalize preservation of the supplied draft. Treat the ambiguity transparently rather than retroactively sharpening the rule.
- Critical-failure rules combine factual/operational failures with categorical boundaries. In particular, distinguish complying with the requested unsupported ranking of people from offering a fair artifact-based assessment. Preserve concrete quoted evidence for each critical classification.
- The scoring section explicitly assigns both evaluators all 16 initial responses but does not specify the same independent scoring/adjudication protocol for H01/H02. Do not silently pool continuation grades or agreement into the initial denominator.

## Reporting boundary

Describe observed case outcomes and four matched differences on this curated suite, with raw denominators and evaluator disagreements. Keep the 16 initial episodes, 12 distinct cases, four comparisons, and two dependent continuations distinct. Any conclusions about broader robustness, user benefit, objective taste/elegance, live-world adoption, or persistent memory remain unsupported. This review does not establish whether the planned execution, masking, hashes, or verification were actually completed.
