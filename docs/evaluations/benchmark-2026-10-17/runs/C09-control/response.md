Keep confirmation and a way to recover. Removing them while mail sometimes fails would make the flow look simpler while leaving volunteers uncertain and coordinators with hidden cleanup.

Make the experience calmer by combining the screens: show a clear on-page signup status, explain that email may take time, and offer a retry or help option when needed. A retry should not create a duplicate signup. Only say “You’re signed up” once the signup is durably recorded; email delivery is a separate status.

Consider removing a separate retry screen once failures are detected and recovery happens reliably without volunteers or coordinators having to chase them. Elegance here means fewer burdens, not merely fewer screens.
